# Hung Vinh Asian Trade

Build a modern, premium B2B wholesale platform for "Hung Vinh Asian Food" (Hung & Vinh Asian Food Pty Ltd) — an Adelaide-based direct importer and wholesale distributor of authentic Vietnamese & Southeast Asian food products, including official ST25 Vilaconic World Champion Rice, traditional sauces, regional cooking bases, tropical beverages, and specialty snacks for restaurants, grocery markets, and food distributors across Australia.

---

### 🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETICS (Modern B2B Food-Trade Luxury)
- **Style Concept:** High-end trade & agricultural export portal. Clean, generous whitespace, subtle glassmorphism header, smooth pill badges, and refined micro-interactions.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary / Brand: Deep Forest Emerald (`#0E3A2F` / `#164E3D`) representing purity, nature, and authenticity.
  - Accent / Gold: Warm Sun-Ripened Harvest Gold (`#C89332` / `#D97706`) for badges, key highlights, and primary CTAs.
  - Canvas / Background: Warm Rice-Alabaster (`#FBF9F5` / `#F4EFE6`) instead of harsh pure white.
  - Text & Accents: Deep Slate/Charcoal (`#1A221E`) with Muted Olive-Sage (`#5F7268`) for subtitles and specs.
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: Elegant Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond`).
  - Body & UI Elements: Clean, crisp Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`).
